Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you register.
  • Expense data — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Usage data — basic information about how you interact with the Service, such as pages visited and features used.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect solely to provide and improve the Service:

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To display your expense history and summaries.
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve app performance.
  • To send essential service communications (e.g. password resets).

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third-party services for marketing or analytics purposes. We may use third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) solely to operate the Service; these providers are bound by confidentiality obligations and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, or would like to request access to or deletion of your data, please contact us at support@spendly.app.
